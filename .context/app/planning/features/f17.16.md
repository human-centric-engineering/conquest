# F17.16 — Routing analytics

> §8 of [`f17-followups.md`](./f17-followups.md), and the smallest gap between "the data exists" and
> "anyone can see it" in the whole phase.

## What was wrong

Every plan is recorded. Every amendment is recorded **twice** — on `InterviewPlan.amendments` and as
a `source: 'respondent'` topic — and the type's own docblock says why: _"deliberately visible to
routing-quality analytics as a SEPARATE signal"_. Nothing read either.

So the two failures that matter most to an adaptive instrument were both invisible:

- **A criteria sentence that never fires.** No error, no empty state, no report. The topic simply
  never appears in anyone's interview, and the instrument quietly asks less than its author believes
  it asks. F17.14's plan preview made this checkable one synthetic opening at a time; nothing said
  it about the interviews that actually ran.
- **A criteria sentence respondents keep correcting.** The plan looks entirely ordinary. Yet a
  respondent asking for a topic the planner left out is the sharpest available evidence that a
  version's criteria are wrong, and it was sitting in a JSON column nobody queried.

## Confirmed decisions

- **A blind-spot sample is not a selection** (added under review). `chooseCheckTopic` is
  deterministic — author preference, else the first unselected conditional in authored order — and
  the check defaults on, so one topic is seated as a `light` sample in nearly every plan _because
  nothing chose it_. Counting that as selection broke both headline findings at once:
  `criteria_never_fires` could never fire for the topic that most needed it, and
  `criteria_always_fires` fired instead, advising the author to promote a topic whose criteria never
  matched. The row now carries `chosen` (`llm` + `rule`) apart from `sampled` (`check`), the
  findings read `chosen`, and the table shows them in separate columns. `fallback` counts as
  neither — it is what happens when there was no signal to judge on at all.

- **The read is bounded and says so** (added under review). The card fetches on mount, so this runs
  on an ordinary Topics-tab visit; `ROUTING_PLAN_READ_CAP` (2,000, newest first) bounds how many
  plan blobs one read pulls into memory, and `truncated` surfaces when it bit rather than presenting
  a sample as the whole.

- **A correction is never counted as a success.** `selected` excludes `source: 'respondent'`
  entirely; the amendment is counted on its own axis. This is the entire reason amendments were
  recorded separately, and getting it wrong would make a version's criteria look better the worse
  they got. Both records are read and unioned — `amendments` is absent on plans written before it
  shipped, where the source tag is the only trace.

- **`excluded` means "left out and stayed out".** `applyAmendment` deletes the topic from the plan's
  `excluded` list, so the two counts partition what the planner did not seat rather than
  double-counting it: `excluded` is the decisions that held, `amended` is the ones overturned.

- **Findings are observations carrying their sample size, not verdicts.** A topic that never fires
  may be a rare-case safety net working exactly as designed, and only the author knows which. Four
  codes: `criteria_never_fires`, `criteria_always_fires`, `respondents_keep_adding`,
  `budget_decides`. Silent below `ROUTING_FINDING_MIN_PLANS` (5), and only ever about **conditional
  topics that still exist** — an always-run topic has no criteria to be wrong, and a deleted one
  cannot be edited.

- **The finding threshold is a separate constant from `K_ANONYMITY_THRESHOLD`**, despite both being
  5 today. One governs disclosure (how few respondents make a count re-identifying), the other
  inference (how few interviews make "it never fires" mean anything). Sharing the constant would let
  a privacy change silently redefine what counts as evidence.

- **Counts and topic keys only.** A plan carries respondent free text twice —
  `amendments[].request` and the `rationale` written from it. The aggregator reads neither, on the
  principle F17.14 settled: an authoring surface is not a place to put respondent answers. Registered
  as a k-anonymity-bearing surface in `anonymous-mode.md`, suppressed via `isCohortSuppressed()`
  (**not** the alpha-bypassing `isAnalyticsPanelSuppressed`, which that doc scopes to the three
  dashboard panels — widening it is a privacy decision, not a side effect of this feature). The
  suppression costs nothing in practice: the finding threshold blanks the same cohorts anyway.
  Suppression zeroes the rows, the findings and every aggregate **except** the cohort size, which
  stays so the surface can say how far off the threshold it is.

- **The card loads itself.** Both failures are invisible by nature, so putting them behind a "show
  me" button would reproduce the problem in miniature — a finding nobody clicks is a finding nobody
  has. It fetches on mount whenever the version is switched on and has a conditional topic, and
  renders nothing at all otherwise.

- **It sits on the Adaptive scope tab, not the analytics dashboard.** The fix for every finding is a
  criteria field three inches away. It is placed immediately after the plan preview deliberately:
  "what it would do" and "what it did" are the same question asked of intent and of evidence.

- **The default 30-day window, like every sibling endpoint.** All-time-per-version is arguably the
  more apt cohort for judging criteria, but consistency with the four analytics routes beside it is
  worth more than a bespoke window, and `from`/`to` are there for anyone who wants otherwise.

## What changed

| Path                                             | Change                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `lib/app/questionnaire/analytics/routing.ts`     | **New.** Pure `assembleRoutingAnalytics` + the `getRoutingAnalytics` DB seam |
| `lib/app/questionnaire/analytics/views.ts`       | `RoutingTopicRow`, `RoutingFinding`, `RoutingAnalyticsResult`                |
| `.../[vid]/analytics/routing/route.ts`           | **New** GET, matching its four siblings exactly                              |
| `components/.../topics/routing-quality-card.tsx` | **New.** Findings lead, counts follow; self-loading                          |
| `components/.../topics/topics-panel.tsx`         | Placed after the plan preview                                                |
| `lib/api/endpoints.ts`                           | `versionAnalyticsRouting`                                                    |
| `.context/app/questionnaire/anonymous-mode.md`   | Registered as a k-anon-bearing surface                                       |

No migration, no new model, no new setting — every number comes from `interviewPlan` blobs that were
already being written.
