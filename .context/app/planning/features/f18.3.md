# F18.3 — The funnel arc

> **Written retrospectively.** This shipped in PR #256 (four phases, `b33ffd701`, `d850f060d`,
> `9fce5b88b`, `3bbe97b68`) without a tracker. The reasoning lives in
> [`interviewer-strategy.md`](../../questionnaire/interviewer-strategy.md) — this records what
> shipped and when.

## What was wrong

The funnel approach — open questions first, narrowing to specifics as coverage builds — ran on four
hard-coded constants. An admin could choose the approach but not how fast it moved, and the help
text ("as coverage builds it steers toward the specific points still missing") could not answer the
question admins actually ask: _is the openness a two-question preamble, or a gradual descent?_

The opening was also entirely the interviewer's own invention. The first thing a respondent is ever
asked decides whether they write three words or three paragraphs, and a client with a particular way
of opening had no way to say so.

## What shipped

**A pace dial** — `gradual` · `balanced` · `brisk` — moving four numbers together as one
`FunnelPaceProfile`: the opening window, the two coverage thresholds, and the no-coverage round
fallback. They move together because they are not independently meaningful; an admin who widened the
open band but left the opening window at one ask would get an arc that contradicts itself.

**`balanced` reproduces the original hard-coded constants boundary for boundary**, pinned by a test
rather than left to good intentions. That is what makes the dial a provable no-op for every
questionnaire that never touches it.

**`FunnelArcExplainer`** renders the bands as a table derived from the _same_ `FUNNEL_PACE_PROFILES`
the runtime reads — showing the arc rather than describing it. A hard-coded explainer that drifted
would be worse than the vague prose it replaced, because the admin would have no reason to doubt it.

**Admin-guided opening questions** (`openingMode`, `openingExamples`) plus an AI suggester that
proposes openers for this questionnaire, and two `info` lints for when the setting is inert.

## Confirmed decisions

- **The pace is honoured for `funnel` only.** `open` and `targeted` always read `balanced`, and the
  editor shows the dial only for `funnel` — a stored pace quietly reshaping an `open` session's
  opening window would be an effect with no visible cause.
- **A terse respondent steps the phase one notch toward targeted.** Broad invitations are not paying
  off, so it gets specific sooner.
- **Percentages go through `Math.round`.** `0.55 * 100` is `55.00000000000001` in IEEE floats and
  `brisk` is the pace that exposes it; a test pins that no percentage renders a decimal point.
- **The arc is enabled by default** (`DEFAULT_INTERVIEWER_STRATEGY.enabled` is `true`), which phase 4
  migrated the column default to match. Every questionnaire runs an arc — which is why the turn
  evaluator and the analytics surface both had to learn about it.
