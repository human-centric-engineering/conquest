# F17.17 — One follow-up for the whole opening (G03)

> §5 of [`f17-followups.md`](./f17-followups.md), and the last of the pilot client's guardrails the
> platform could not express.

## What was wrong

The client's guardrail G03 says three things, and we had none of them:

1. Probe only when an answer is **too abstract to route** — "a predictable revenue engine".
2. Do **not** probe when it is already routable — "reps who cannot hold a CFO conversation".
3. **One probe for the whole opening**, because every probe costs a section of the time budget.

What existed was `maxDataSlotAttempts`: a **per-slot** re-ask cap. That is the wrong unit. A per-slot
cap has no idea a follow-up was already spent three questions ago, so an opening with five slots and
a cap of two could spend five follow-ups and still be within its limits. Nothing anywhere was
session-scoped, and nothing asked whether the follow-up was worth asking.

The tracker called the two halves separable, and they are: the counter is cheap, the classifier is
the part that needs care — because spending the opening's only probe on an answer that was _already_
specific enough is the failure G03 actually names.

## What it does

`adaptiveScope.limitOpeningProbes` (off) + `adaptiveScope.maxOpeningProbes` (1, range 0–5). When
on, a data slot belonging to an `opening` topic gets an effective re-ask cap of
`1` when the allowance is spent or the answer is already routable, and the author's own
`maxDataSlotAttempts` otherwise. Before any probe is spent, `assessOpeningRoutability` asks whether
the plan could already be decided from what has been said.

## Decisions worth keeping

- **The mechanism is the existing park, not a new branch.** Lowering the cap means a probe the
  opening cannot afford becomes an ordinary move-on: a provisional fill and a bridge to a fresh
  area. The runtime already knew how to do that and already had tests for it. A new "declined to
  probe" response kind would have been a second way for an interview to stall.

- **The cap is only ever lowered, so it can only subtract questions.** An allowance of five cannot
  make an interview ask a sixth time when the author's per-slot cap says two — a slot already at
  that cap fails the `attempts < cap` guard and never consults the allowance. A guardrail that could
  _add_ questions would be a strange guardrail.

- **The allowance is counted down exactly once, by `spent`.** The first revision also lowered the cap
  to `1 + remaining` on the affordable path, which double-counts: the follow-up being authorised now
  increments `spent`, so the next turn's `remaining` is already one lower and parks the slot then.
  An author who set three probes silently got two. Caught by `/code-review`, not by the tests — at
  `maxOpeningProbes: 1` the two arithmetics agree, and every first-round test used 1 (or held
  `spent: 0` constant). The regression test now walks the real sequence with the allowance at 3.

- **Two settings fields, not one.** `sessionBudgetSeconds` uses `0` to mean "off" because zero
  seconds is not a budget anyone means. `0` follow-ups **is** a setting someone means ("never probe
  in the opening"), so it cannot also be the off switch. The switch is its own boolean.

- **The classifier runs on the planner's own agent.** The check is a prediction of the planner's own
  judgement — "could you already choose?" — so asking a _different_ agent would let it disagree with
  the planner about the very question it exists to anticipate. The tier differs (`routing`, not
  `reasoning`) and so does the timeout (6s, not 12s), because this one runs inside a live turn while
  the respondent watches a typing indicator.

- **`routable: null` is not `routable: false`.** Every failure — no agent, no provider, a timeout,
  garbled JSON — returns null, and the caller then spends the probe, exactly as the interview would
  have before this feature existed. The check may only ever save a question. Making a missing model
  call _skip_ a question would have been the one genuinely unsafe direction.

- **`spent` is derived from the turn record, never stored.** Opening-slot turns minus distinct
  opening slots. Self-healing where a counter column is not: a turn that never persisted never spent
  a probe. It also catches a _return_ to a slot the interview had moved on from, which costs the
  respondent exactly as much as a consecutive re-ask.

- **Read over the full turn history, not the loader's window.** `RECENT_TURNS_WINDOW` bounds the
  transcript the loader already holds; an allowance counted off that window would silently refill
  itself on a long opening. One narrow extra query, and only for the turns where the allowance can
  actually bind.

- **The check is never paid for when there is nothing to decide.** Not on a first ask, not on a
  covered slot, not when the allowance is exhausted, and not when the version has no conditional
  topics to be routable against.

- **The card renumbered rather than appending.** The Adaptive scope settings are numbered in
  **decision order**, which is the whole reason the cap reads as enforced rather than requested. The
  opening happens before the decision, so it is step 1 and everything else moved down. Appending it
  as step 6 would have quietly broken the one thing the numbering is for.

## What was deliberately left

- **Nothing prices the interviewer's own turns.** C7 prices the _questions_; a probe is a turn.
  Bounding probes by count is the honest thing to enforce until turns are measured — the same reason
  `maxConditionalTopics` is a count.
- **A running interview is never re-planned**, and this does not change that. The allowance bounds
  what the opening asks, not what the plan later costs.
- **Form questions in the opening are not rationed.** The interviewer re-asks data slots, not form
  questions. Rather than pretend otherwise, `opening_probe_limit_inert` says so on the tab.

| Path                                                           | Change                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `lib/app/questionnaire/scope/probe.ts`                         | **New.** The counter — pure, so the orchestrator can import it without pulling in Prisma |
| `lib/app/questionnaire/scope/routability.ts`                   | **New.** "Could the plan already be decided?" — null on every failure                    |
| `lib/app/questionnaire/scope/types.ts`                         | `limitOpeningProbes` / `maxOpeningProbes`, their bounds, defaults and narrowing          |
| `lib/app/questionnaire/scope/schemas.ts`                       | The two fields on the settings patch                                                     |
| `lib/app/questionnaire/scope/validate.ts`                      | `opening_probe_limit_inert` / `opening_probe_limit_moot`, and the optional cap input     |
| `lib/app/questionnaire/orchestrator/data-slot-orchestrator.ts` | The effective cap at the park gate, plus `OPENING_PROBE_TOOL_SLUG` on the turn record    |
| `lib/app/questionnaire/orchestrator/types.ts`                  | `TurnState.openingProbe`, `assessOpeningRoutability`, `OpeningRoutabilityOutcome`        |
| `app/api/v1/app/questionnaires/_lib/turn-context.ts`           | Resolves the allowance — gated, one query                                                |
| `app/api/v1/app/questionnaires/_lib/topic-routes.ts`           | `loadMaxDataSlotAttempts`, read only so the tab can flag a limit that cannot bind        |
| `.../questionnaire-sessions/_lib/turn-invokers.ts`             | Wires the check, and only when the route supplies candidate topics                       |
| `.../questionnaire-sessions/[id]/messages/route.ts`            | Supplies them, gated on the allowance being present                                      |
| `components/.../topics/scope-settings-card.tsx`                | Step 1 — the switch, the number, and the help that says what a follow-up costs           |
| `lib/app/questionnaire/settings-registry.ts`                   | One row: off, "never probe", or the allowance                                            |

No migration, no new model, no new agent: the allowance is config, the count is derived from turns
already written, and the check reuses the planner's binding.

## Tests

- `tests/unit/lib/app/questionnaire/scope/probe.test.ts` — what a probe _is_: the second and every
  later turn on the same opening slot, including a return after moving on, ignoring turns that
  targeted nothing or something outside the opening; and a remainder that never goes negative
- `tests/unit/lib/app/questionnaire/scope/routability.test.ts` — the fail-soft contract, each
  failure mode returning null rather than a verdict, and that it asks the planner's own binding
- `tests/unit/lib/app/questionnaire/orchestrator/data-slot-orchestrator.test.ts` — the gate: parks
  when spent, withholds when already routable, probes when abstract, probes when the check could not
  be made, never touches a slot outside the opening, never raises the author's cap, never gates a
  first ask or a covered slot
- `tests/unit/lib/app/questionnaire/scope/validate.test.ts` — both ways a limit can be set and bind
  nothing, and that neither is reported while Adaptive Scope is off
- `tests/unit/lib/app/questionnaire/scope/settings.test.ts` — off by default; `0` survives narrowing
  because "never probe" is a real setting
- `tests/integration/api/v1/app/questionnaires/turn-context.test.ts` — the allowance resolved and
  counted, and the extra query not made for anyone else
