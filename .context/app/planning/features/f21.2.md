# F21.2 — The runtime

> Phase B of [the P21 plan](./f21-sectioned-interviews.md). The conversation is now bounded to one
> section at a time, each section opens with its own opening question, and a section can be closed
> and reopened. Shipped in the same commit as [F21.3](./f21.3.md).

## What shipped

- `sections/state.ts` — `buildSectionState`, called from `buildTurnContext`. **The one place
  sections are decided.** It resolves, reconciles the stored run, picks the active section and
  assesses its close gate.
- `sections/run.ts` — `SectionRun`, its narrower, and the pure transitions (`openSection`,
  `closeSection`, `recordTurnInSection`, `reconcileSectionRun`).
- Section-bounded targeting in **both** orchestrators, a per-section opening question and funnel-arc
  reset, and `POST /questionnaire-sessions/:id/sections` with `{ action: 'open' | 'close', key }`.
- Two nullable columns: `AppQuestionnaireSession.sectionRun`, `AppQuestionnaireTurn.sectionKey`. No
  backfill; every pre-P21 row reads correctly as-is.

## The four things that were defects first

### 1. The bound is a SECOND list, never a narrowing of the first

The obvious implementation narrows `base.questions` and `base.dataSlots` at the choke point and lets
everything downstream inherit the bound. Those two lists are also read by the **submit gate**, the
weighted coverage, the progress bar and the milestone ledger. Narrowed, a session offered to submit
the moment its FIRST section was covered, and the bar read 100% with six sections still to come.

> Read `sectionQuestions` where a question is **chosen**. Read `questions` wherever anything is
> **measured**.

### 2. The same mistake, one level down, in the selector

`selectNext` is handed the section's questions, and `terminalDecision` re-derives coverage from
whatever list it is given. So at the end of every section it returned `complete`, and the respondent
was told "that's everything we need" with every later part still to come — on default settings.

The fix separates a section-scoped terminal verdict from a real one by asking `terminalDecision`
over the WHOLE interview: `null` there means only the part ended, and the reply becomes a
`section_covered` response naming it. Asking that question, rather than trusting "we are sectioned",
is what keeps `maxQuestionsPerSession` terminating for real. It also gave `agentOffersClose` its
first runtime consumer; it had been schema-only.

### 3. The empty-run fallback banked a run with no entries

`openSection` and `recordTurnInSection` both map over EXISTING entries, so the turn route's fallback
wrote a run with an empty `sections` array. The turn went uncharged, and `maxTurnsPerSection` — the
one thing that can release a respondent stuck on a required question they will not answer — could
never fire. It seeds through `reconcileSectionRun` now.

### 4. Reconciliation runs every turn, not just at the start

Conditional Topics seats new topics when the plan lands, and a respondent amendment can add one
later still, so sections genuinely appear mid-interview. An entry whose section stopped resolving is
**kept and appended** rather than dropped, so turns already tagged with it are not orphaned.

## Why `turnsSpent` is counted, not derived

Under `free` navigation a respondent's turns in a section are not contiguous. Deriving the figure
from `openedAtTurn` and the current ordinal would charge section 3's turns to section 1's budget,
and `maxTurnsPerSection` is measured against it.

## Why reopening needed no machinery

The session-level reopen needed `isReopenEligible` and its own seam because it crosses a terminal
status. A section has none: `closed` is a position in a run, not an end state, so reopening it is a
plain write.
